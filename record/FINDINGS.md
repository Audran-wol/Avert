## Data conflicts worth preserving

- Douala date windows differ between initial news and later humanitarian coverage.
- Bagre release notices change, and some reported levels/arithmetic are inconsistent. Store dated advisories; obtain SONABEL/WRC statements before treating releases as observations.
- Ghana national June figures change across reporting dates and definitions. “Affected”, “displaced”, “households” and “all rain-related deaths” are not interchangeable. The August breakdown does not fully reconcile with its headline totals.
- A media filename/upload month is not necessarily its capture date. The article publication date must remain a different field.
- Repeated coordinates in a photographer’s collection may be metadata copied across files; they are not ground-survey precision.
